ABOUT THE PROJECT

关于这个项目

一本名为《雷格纳斯漫游指南》的旅行杂志， 记录游戏里的风景和那些被人遗忘的角落，给未来留下一份纪念。

A small travel guide, documenting the game's landscapes and forgotten corners as a memento for the future.

SUBMISSIONS

投稿

只要是游戏里你觉得值得截图留念的地方，都可以投给我！ 不论是好看的风景、偶然撞见的小惊喜，还是你私藏的某个角落，全都欢迎 ~

Any place in the game that you think is worth a screenshot is welcome! It could be a cool view, some random spot you found by chance, or just your personal favorite corner. Everything counts!

不用特意为了这次投稿去现拍，以前存的旧截图也完全没问题！ Feel free to take new photos, or just send in older screenshots from your gallery. Both are great!

投稿时请告诉我 Required Information

01 署名 / Credit 请留下你的名字或希望使用的名字。
Please provide your preferred name (Any name you like)
02 地点 / Location 请告诉我照片的具体拍摄地点。
Please tell me the location where the photo was shot.

地点可以截图小地图，或者告诉我附近的传送点、地标等明显的参照物。 我需要根据地点整理这些照片。

Just make sure to let me know where it is! You can drop a screenshot of your minimap, or just tell me the nearest waypoint or landmark so I don't get lost trying to group everything.
